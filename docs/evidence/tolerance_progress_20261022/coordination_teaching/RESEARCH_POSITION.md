# Research scope after coordination teaching

The governing objective is the later STTW-CORE-OBJECTIVE in the shared ledger: one alpha-conditioned middle policy coordinates speed and steering references over frozen local300, expresses speed/path preference, and recovers the original task after disturbance. The original two-network derivation guide is historical context; it does not require reverting the current single-network implementation.

## What the new experiment answers

The six TRAIN examples show that alternative correction timing can recover states where the parent fails. A supervised update to the same Actor transfers some behavior to ordinary closed-loop execution without a mask or new selector. Fixed-alpha preference survives the original nominal DEV panel; low-speed and strong-pulse task recovery improve, with increased roll in some cases. This directly addresses the task/coordination objective.

The evidence does not show that the analytic tolerance formula caused this improvement. Formula reward and guided/shuffled/uniform sampling experiments remain separate, including their negative results. Do not rename teaching as tolerance optimization or retrofit an explanation into a causal claim.

## What remains before stronger claims

1. Optimization-seed repetition is currently running. Shared parent/dataset/reset means this checks minibatch-seed sensitivity, not independent dataset or environment replication.
2. Lock a new geometry/speed/alpha/disturbance panel after the candidate and protocol are fixed, before inspecting outcomes. Report original-task recovery, continuous errors, peak roll and alpha preference. Avoid checkpoint reselection on this panel.
3. Keep tolerance as a policy-specific directional diagnostic unless a matched experiment supports extra utility. Any new empirical margin evaluation must identify the closed-loop policy and distinguish recovery from stricter working performance. A finite observed recovery boundary is not a physical or certified safe limit.

Online alpha switching has not been evaluated and must not appear as a demonstrated capability. No new input, safety governor, predictor network, reward version or low-level controller change is needed to describe the present result. The unchanged interface is itself useful engineering evidence, but novelty requires comparison with related methods rather than claiming that preference conditioning or imitation is new.
