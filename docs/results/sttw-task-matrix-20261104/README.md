# STTW matched route matrix: complete simulation inventory

This is an exploratory, state-matched simulation matrix for the RAS-style [article](../../paper/MANUSCRIPT.pdf). All **504 physical rollouts** completed their declared 10 s horizon: H75 216, U170 216, and zero-middle Pure Pursuit (PP) 72. All raw trajectories, including non-arrivals, are saved under [`task_matrix_v2_20261104`](/media/qy/6A7E3DB07E3D75C3/STTW_CONTROL_archives/tolerance_calibration_disk_recovery_20261025/task_matrix_v2_20261104). There were zero optimizer updates in this evaluation. The article presents H75 as the previously selected main middle policy, U170 as a continued learned middle policy, and PP as an established geometric follower sharing the same frozen lower balance layer. U170 is not an independent training seed.

## Experiment and measures

At each requested speed (1.90, 2.50, 3.05 m/s), six routes cover left/right single turns and left/right-leading S turns. The low and high sets include tighter and wider single-turn radii. The medium set includes exact left/right 90-degree **polyline vertices**, without a smoothed reference arc. Every original goal is 7 m after the final bend or vertex, with a 1 m entry disk. Two complete prepared initial-state banks (2 and 5) are restored across methods. Each condition is run without a force and with a +36 N world-Y pulse from 2.80 to 2.95 s. The 10 Hz causal reconnection planner, 200 Hz lower controller, vehicle/contact parameters and actuator interface are shared.

For H75 and U170, fixed preferences are alpha=0, 0.5 and 1. PP has exactly zero middle latent output and no alpha-dependent physical rollout. The corrected PP raw trajectories were checked for zero `latent_z`; the earlier mislabeled nonzero-middle PP run was removed and is described in [`INVALID_COMPARATOR_REMOVAL.md`](/media/qy/6A7E3DB07E3D75C3/STTW_CONTROL_archives/tolerance_calibration_disk_recovery_20261025/task_matrix_v2_20261104/INVALID_COMPARATOR_REMOVAL.md). None of that invalid run enters the article or results below.

The common outcome measures are original-goal entry, contact/fault, full-horizon completion, actual forward speed, original-path cross-track MAE/RMSE, maximum original-path excursion and peak roll. Bend statistics use the original-progress interval spanning the turn through 2 m after its end. All metrics come from actual motion projected to the **original** route; the execution reconnection does not redefine the scoring route. Each per-case record gives the raw NPZ path and SHA256. The fixed world force can have different body-lateral directions along left/right geometry.

All 504 cases ran to the 10 s observation horizon; none recorded contact failure or controller fault. H75 entered the original goal in 212/216 cases, U170 in 209/216, and PP in 72/72. Original-goal entry does not certify corridor compliance or sustained tracking. PP has lower aggregate bend MAE in every speed/force stratum than any H75 alpha setting. H75's higher speed preference generally increases actual bend speed and error. On the **low-speed left-leading S turn with pulse**, H75 α1 has slightly higher bend speed and smaller bend MAE than PP in both prepared states; this post-hoc selected positive example is illustrated in the article and is not representative of the full matrix. For the four medium-speed sharp-corner route/state nominal conditions, PP enters 4/4, H75 α0/.5/1 enters 4/4, 4/4, 3/4, and U170 enters 4/4, 2/4, 2/4. Every missing entry remains in the raw inventory.

## Files and reproducibility

The [H75 analysis](/media/qy/6A7E3DB07E3D75C3/STTW_CONTROL_archives/tolerance_calibration_disk_recovery_20261025/task_matrix_v2_20261104/analysis_h75_full/README.md) and [U170 analysis](/media/qy/6A7E3DB07E3D75C3/STTW_CONTROL_archives/tolerance_calibration_disk_recovery_20261025/task_matrix_v2_20261104/analysis_u170_full/README.md) each contain `summary.json`, all per-case metrics in `case_metrics.json`, and matched original/actual XY, actual-speed and roll figures for every route/state/event. PP cases appear in both analyses as the same 72 physical trajectories; they are counted once in the 504 total. Each of the nine `low|mid|high` × `h75|u170|pp_corrected` run directories contains `plan.json`, completion status, case index, raw per-case NPZs and original-path NPZs. The resolved run plan, not just the short repository configuration, fixes the exact physical and controller contract.

Repository source: [`native_goal_run.py`](../../../learning/src/sttw_control/native_goal_run.py) generates and evaluates the declared route matrix; [`contact_only_environment.py`](../../../learning/src/sttw_control/contact_only_environment.py) holds the shared contact-only environment; the nine [`task_matrix_v2_*.json`](../../../learning/configs/) files select the run conditions; [`task_matrix_v2.py`](../../paper/analysis/task_matrix_v2.py) recomputes every measure and all XY panels; [`make_matrix_figures.py`](../../paper/analysis/make_matrix_figures.py) and [`make_matrix_table.py`](../../paper/analysis/make_matrix_table.py) create the article assets. The middle reward, PPO objective and actor architecture are documented in [`method_training.tex`](../../paper/method_training.tex) and their frozen training configurations; evaluation applies **no new reward or training update**. The earlier five-alpha H75 nominal study and development-selected curvature-speed rule are a separate historical dataset in [`sttw-tolerance-20261101`](../sttw-tolerance-20261101/README.md), with a different comparator and metric definition.

The route matrix was designed after earlier development evidence, uses two prepared states and one world-fixed disturbance, and is not an independent locked TEST. It is simulation evidence only. No tolerance predictor, online preference coordinator or hardware trial is claimed here.
