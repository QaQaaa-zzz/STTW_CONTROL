Signed 36 N world-fixed force pulses begin at 2.8 s and last 0.15 s. U170 and the development-frozen rule use identical four routes, two prepared banks, local200 lower controller, friction 0.8, H10 and original-path task score. This extension was specified after the nominal result and is exploratory.

The positive matched example is the `F+36` pulse on the low-speed right S turn and high-speed right single turn, each with banks 2/5. U170 at alpha=1 completed the wide task in all four cases; U170 at alpha=0 and the geometric rule completed none. On the low-speed S turn, alpha=1 retained a mean turn speed of 1.789/1.824 m/s in banks 2/5, versus 1.638/1.642 m/s at alpha=0. The [paper panel](../../../paper/figures/positive_pulse_xy.png) shows original/actual XY and measured speed for both low-speed states. This subset was identified after opening the full signed matrix; the full table and all per-case rows below remain the scope of inference.
